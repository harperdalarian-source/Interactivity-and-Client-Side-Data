Classes and Private Sessions

Choose a practice based on your experience, energy level, and what you need that day. Every class is designed to feel welcoming and easy to understand.

Gentle Yoga

A slower class with clear instruction and time to settle into each movement. This is a welcoming option for beginners and anyone who prefers a softer pace.

Vinyasa

A more active class that connects movement with breathing. Students can follow a steady sequence while working at their own pace.

Restorative Yoga

A quiet practice focused on slowing down, resting, and releasing some of the tension that builds up during a busy week.

Private Sessions

One-on-one time to ask questions, become more comfortable with the basics, or focus on a personal goal.
