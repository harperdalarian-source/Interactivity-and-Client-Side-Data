About River Bend Yoga

River Bend Yoga is meant to feel supportive instead of intimidating. The studio welcomes people who are brand new to yoga as well as people who want a steady practice they can return to during a busy season of life.

Our Instructors

Instructors focus on clear directions, comfortable pacing, and helping new students understand what to expect in class.

Accessibility and Class Support

Students are encouraged to ask about accessibility needs before a class or event. The studio can explain class options, pacing, and what to expect before attending.

Studio Policies

Please arrive with enough time to settle in, respect the quiet shared space, and let the instructor know if you have a question or need an adjustment.
